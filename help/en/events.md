**Events** keeps your dates: courses, concerts, open days. Each event
has a beginning, if you like an end, a title, a place, a short teaser,
a text, an image and categories.

## Show the events on the website

A page shows the events when an admin chooses it in its settings under
**This page shows**, e.g. on a page called "Events". The upcoming events
appear there from the next one on, page by page, with a link to the past
ones. Each event gets its own address below that page, e.g.
`/events/roasting-course`, and the event shows there: with
its time and place, a map once the visitor agrees, and a link to put it
into a calendar.

An event counts as upcoming until it is over: until its end, else its
beginning. Events all day long last until the end of their last day.
The list moves on by itself.

For separate events, such as courses and parties, an admin adds another
**space** with its own page there.

The block **Next events** shows the next few, e.g. on the start page,
from all spaces or from one, with a link to all of them.

## Create an event

Choose **New event**, write the title, set the beginning and save. The
event stays a draft until you choose **Publish**. Changes to a
published event are a draft as well, visitors see them once you
publish again.

- **Ends** stays empty if the end is open.
- **All day long** shows only the days, e.g. for a festival over a
  weekend.
- **Venue** is the name of the place, **Street and town** its address.
- **Coordinates** put a marker on the map, e.g. "53.5444, 9.9663".
  Right-click the place in a map to copy them.

Under **Plan** you set a time when the draft goes online by itself, or
when the event goes offline.

## Categories

Under **Categories** you create topics such as "Courses" or "Concerts",
also below each other. Visitors can filter the list by them. Deleting a
category keeps its events.

## Tags

Tick the tags that fit or type new words, separated by commas. The
event shows its tags below the text, and each leads to the list of all
events with that tag. A teaser of the next events can show only those
with one tag.

## Courses with booking

Courses whose seats and bookings live at a provider, such as Avaro,
come in with a module of the provider. They are not events of this
screen: dates, prices and free seats are always the current ones of
the provider, and bookings go straight there. Three blocks show them:

- **Course with dates** on the page of a course: choose the course,
  write the text, and the block adds the price per attendee, the least
  and most attendees and the coming dates with free seats.
- **Courses soon** shows the next dates, e.g. on the start page. A date
  that cannot be booked online says why: sold out, booking opens soon,
  or booking by phone or in person.
- **Course booking** is the form. Put it on a page of its own, e.g.
  "Booking"; the dates link to the first page with it. A visitor fills
  in their details, the attendees and the questions of the course,
  agrees to the legal texts and books. The confirmation comes from the
  provider by email.

Free seats on the website may lag up to a minute behind; the provider
checks them again for each booking.
